Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

KitKat is a personal AI assistant project. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when using KitKat and its connected services.

Information We Access

Depending on the features being used, KitKat may access information provided directly by the user or information made available through connected services, such as Instagram.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the features requested by the user, operate the assistant, and maintain the functionality of connected services.

Instagram Information

When Instagram features are enabled, KitKat may access information made available through the Instagram APIs, such as account information, messages, media, or comments, according to the permissions granted to the application.

Data Sharing

KitKat does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide an enabled service or where required by law.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of information associated with KitKat. Instructions are available on the User Data Deletion page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or deletion requests, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with this application.
