User Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with the KitKat application.

How to Request Deletion

Send a deletion request to the developer using the contact information associated with this application. Please include enough information to identify the relevant account or data without sending passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

Instagram Data

If KitKat has accessed information through Instagram, the deletion request may include Instagram-related information associated with the application.

Processing Requests

Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed as applicable, subject to legal or security requirements that may require certain information to be retained.
